PRO TIPS
🎯
OBJECTIVE
First to 11 points wins. Get the ball past the CPU paddle on the right side.
🏓
MOVEMENT
W / S
Move your paddle up and down. Arrow Keys also work.
💡 Where you hit on the paddle changes the ball angle — see the ANGLE tab!
🌀
SPIN
Moving your paddle while hitting adds spin — the ball curves mid-flight.
💡 Flick up/down just before contact for trickshots the CPU can't read.
🔥
COMBO
5 hits = ×2 coins · 10 hits = ×3 · 15 hits = ×4 · 20 hits = ×5 ULTRA. Coin rewards multiply with your streak!
🎰
ARENA EVENTS
Every ~25s a random event fires: ⚡ Extra ball · 🟢 Giant CPU · 😬 Tiny CPU. 3s warning, lasts 8s.
🤖
CPU PERSONALITY
ROOKIE (slow) · SNIPER (precise) · TRICKSTER (random jitter) · PRO (fast + accurate). Watch the badge!
⏸
PAUSE
ESC
Pause the game. ESC again to open the quit menu.
ANGLE CONTROL
Where the ball hits your paddle controls its launch angle:

▸ Hit near top edge
  → ball shoots sharply upward

▸ Hit near bottom edge
  → ball shoots sharply downward

▸ Hit dead center
  → straight shot

CHARGE SHOT
Hold SPACE to charge, release to fire.
3 power levels cycle in the demo:

▸ Quick tap — Smash (1.6× speed)

▸ ~66% charge — Power shot (2.1×) + slow-mo flash

▸ Full charge — Piercing ball (2.9×) passes straight through bumpers!

Watch the paddle glow:
white → orange → red

↺ CYCLING DEMO
DIAMOND BUMPER
Press E to place a diamond-shaped bumper just ahead of your paddle.

The ball hits any face and deflects 90° — creating angles the CPU can't anticipate.

Lasts 5 seconds · 6s cooldown

💡 Aim the 90° deflect toward CPU's corner for a near-guaranteed point.

↺ LOOPING DEMO
PORTALS
4 portals on the top and bottom walls. Ball enters one and exits from its linked partner instantly.

▸ Purple portals connect to each other
▸ Gold portals connect to each other

Links shift every 15 seconds through 3 states. A warning flashes 2s before each shift.

💡 Portal paths the CPU can't cover in time = free points.

⬛
SHRINK CPU
CPU paddle shrinks to ~half height for 7s. Aim for the corners!
🐌
SLOW BALL
Ball speed drops to 55%. Use the breathing room to set up a charge shot.
✕2
MULTI-BALL
Spawns a second ball. CPU has to track both — aim one at each edge to force a miss.
↕
REVERSE CPU
CPU tracks an inverted ball position — it moves the wrong way!
🔽
SPEED DOWN
Cuts ball speed by 50%. Stacks well after SLOW BALL.
⚡
ELECTRIC BALL
Ball turns electric. First hit paralyzes the paddle it touches for 3.5s.
💡 Orb tags: S · SL · MB · RV · SD · EL — spot the type at a glance.
🎯 ANGLE TRAP
Send the ball to the TOP corner, then charge shot. CPU predicts center hits — the sharp angle catches it off-guard.
💎 DIAMOND REDIRECT
Drop the diamond so its 90° deflect points straight at CPU's corner. Creates a shot the AI simply can't reach.
🌀 PORTAL EXPLOIT
In State B (P1↔P3), ball entering top-left exits bottom-right — crossing the whole arena diagonally in an instant.
⚡ ELECTRIC + PIERCE
Get Electric Ball power-up, then full charge. Ball passes bumpers AND paralyzes CPU on contact.
🔥 FIRE STREAK
Score 2 goals in a row → ON FIRE mode. Every goal after that earns bonus coins. Keep the pressure on!
🤖 READ THE AI
ROOKIE barely tracks the ball. TRICKSTER overshoots. Learn when a weak AI spawns and take the round early.
